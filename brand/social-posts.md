## Quick rules
- **Reels/Shorts beat photos** for farming audiences. A 15-second screen recording of Crop Doctor
  diagnosing a real leaf will outperform any graphic here.
- **Post in Hindi too.** Re-post 2, 3 and 5 in Hindi as separate posts — that's most of
  your actual audience.
- **Facebook groups matter more than your own page** early on. District-level and
  crop-specific farming groups are where post 10 belongs.
- **Never promise yield numbers.** Keep the KVK disclaimer on any post about treatment.
